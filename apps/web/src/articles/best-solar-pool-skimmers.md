## What a solar powered pool skimmer is for

A solar powered pool skimmer is a floating robot that sits on the water, runs on a solar panel plus a battery, and collects what lands on the surface: leaves, pollen, insects, hair, small twigs. It does nothing for the floor, the walls or the waterline. Every one of the four below says so or shows it.

That makes it the one robotic pool skimmer purchase that does not compete with a cleaner. A floor robot deals with leaves after they have sunk, stained and started to break down. A skimmer takes them off first. If your pool sits under trees, the strongest setup is both, and the [best robotic pool cleaners](/best-robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/) page covers the other half.

## Solar skimmer or robotic pool cleaner?

Buy a skimmer when the problem is what lands on the water. Buy a cleaner when the problem is the floor, the walls or the waterline ring.

- **A skimmer** floats, runs for most of the day, and removes debris before it sinks. It cannot reach the bottom, so it will not touch sand, silt or algae. Our [Betta SE Plus review](/robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/betta-se-plus/) makes this argument in full.
- **A cleaner** runs a cycle of a couple of hours on the floor and walls and then stops. It collects debris that has already sunk. The [cordless](/best-robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/cordless/) and [above-ground](/best-robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/above-ground-pools/) shortlists rank those.
- **Both** is the answer for most pools under trees, because the skimmer stops the cleaner from inheriting a floor full of leaf mush. Two machines also lets each be cheaper than a single machine that tries to do everything.

## What to compare on a skimmer

Four numbers decide most of it, and none of them is how long the marketing says it runs.

- **Runtime on a grey week.** A battery rated for 30 or 35 hours is refilled by a panel the size of a tray. Ask what it does after three days of cloud. All four makers claim round-the-clock cleaning, and none of those figures is ours.
- **Basket volume and mesh.** A larger basket means fewer trips to empty it. A finer mesh catches pollen and clogs sooner. Only the Beatbot iSkim states a volume (9 litres); Betta states a 200 micron mesh and Aiper 150 micron.
- **How it gets out of trouble.** Steps and ledges strand a floating machine. Sensors, guide wheels and adjustable columns are all answers, and they are not equally proven. The Aiper and the BRINBO both describe adjustable anti-stranding parts on the underside.
- **How you get it out of the water.** Some park themselves at the edge; some need a pole.

## Saltwater, and the app

Two of the four say something about salt. The Betta SE Plus describes twin Salt Chlorine Tolerant motors, and the Beatbot iSkim listing says saltwater-safe. The Aiper and BRINBO listings we read say nothing either way, so check before putting one in a salt pool.

On control, the Beatbot, Aiper and BRINBO listings all show an app. The Betta SE Plus has none, and runs automatically or from a remote. An app matters most when you want to park the machine or see its battery; a skimmer that runs itself needs it least.

## Which one for which pool

- **A pool that sheds a lot, heavy tree cover.** The [Beatbot iSkim](/robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/beatbot-iskim/): the largest stated basket and a covered intake that Beatbot says keeps debris in.
- **A pool with steps, ledges or awkward corners.** The [Aiper EcoSurfer S2](/robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/aiper-ecosurfer-s2/): two dToF sensors and adjustable anti-stranding columns, both described in its listing.
- **A buyer who wants to read the manual first.** The [Betta SE Plus](/robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/betta-se-plus/): the only one here with a manufacturer page, a stated warranty and published charge times.
- **A buyer who wants an app and will accept a newer brand.** The [BRINBO SK01](/robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/brinbo-sk01/): an app, two speeds and a stated adapter charge, from a brand with 28 ratings.
- **A pool in permanent shade.** None of them. A solar skimmer needs sun to refill its battery, and the adapter is a workaround, not a plan.

## What none of the four tells you

Three things are missing from every listing we read, and they are the ones to look for in owner reviews on Amazon before you buy: how long the machine runs after several cloudy days, how often the basket needs emptying in a real pool, and what happens after the warranty. Only Betta states a warranty term. For the rest, assume nothing.
