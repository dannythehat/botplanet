## What a solar pool skimmer is for

A solar pool skimmer is a floating robot that sits on the water, runs on a solar panel and a battery, and collects what lands on the surface: leaves, pollen, insects, hair. It does nothing for the floor, the walls or the waterline, and every one of the four below says so or shows it.

That makes it the one robotic pool skimmer purchase that does not compete with a cleaner. A floor robot deals with leaves after they have sunk and stained. A skimmer takes them off first. If your pool sits under trees, the strongest setup is both, and the [best robotic pool cleaners](/best-robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/) page covers the other half.

## What to compare on a skimmer

Four numbers decide most of it, and none of them is how long the marketing says it runs.

- **Runtime on a grey week.** A battery rated for 30 or 35 hours is refilled by a panel the size of a tray. Ask what it does after three days of cloud.
- **Basket volume and mesh.** A larger basket means fewer trips to empty it. A finer mesh catches pollen and clogs sooner.
- **How it gets out of trouble.** Steps and ledges strand a floating machine. Sensors, guide wheels and adjustable columns are all answers, and they are not equally proven.
- **How you get it out of the water.** Some park themselves; some need a pole.

## Saltwater, and the app

Two of the four say something about salt. The Betta SE Plus describes twin Salt Chlorine Tolerant motors, and the Beatbot iSkim listing says saltwater-safe. The Aiper and BRINBO listings we read say nothing either way, so check before putting one in a salt pool.

On control, the Beatbot, Aiper and BRINBO listings all show an app. The Betta SE Plus has none, and runs automatically or from a remote.

## Get the answer for your own pool

If the floor is your problem, none of these is the answer; start with the cleaners. If the surface is, pick on the question that matters to your pool: a manufacturer you can read, a big basket, a machine built around edges and steps, or a new brand with an app.
