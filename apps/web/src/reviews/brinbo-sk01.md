**The short answer.** The BRINBO SK01 is the newest and least proven of the four solar skimmers we cover. It has the best rating of the four, 4.7 stars, but from 28 ratings against thousands for the Aiper and Betta. It floats, skims the surface and does nothing to the floor, the walls or the waterline.

Everything below about what it does comes from the listing and its pictures. We have not run one. BRINBO is not a name with a track record in pool robots, and that is the first thing to weigh.

---

## Who this is for

Someone who wants an app on a solar skimmer and is comfortable buying a newer brand. The listing shows an SK01 app with a battery reading, a water temperature, a park button and a pause button, which is more control than the simplest skimmers offer.

## Who should look elsewhere

Anyone who values a long record of owner reports, or whose pool needs a floor, wall or waterline cleaner. See the [best robotic pool cleaners](/best-robots/robotic-pool-cleaners/) for those. If you want a skimmer with years of ratings, the Aiper and the Betta have them.

## What it picks up

BRINBO lists leaves, dust, hair and twigs. The machine floats with a large panel on top and two propellers at the front, and a wide intake sits between its floats. The filter is described only as large, with no mesh figure or volume.

## Charging

Solar charging plus a wall adapter, which BRINBO says takes 2.5 hours. A mains adapter is the practical backup for a dull week, and the charge time is a figure the Aiper and Beatbot listings do not give in what we read. Runtime on a charge is not stated in what we read, so we cannot say how long it runs once the battery is full.

## The app

The listing shows an SK01 app with a battery reading, a water temperature, a park button and a pause button, and a gear control for speed. Whether the app is reliable is not something a listing can show.

## Avoiding obstacles

The machine uses ultrasonic sensing to avoid obstacles, which BRINBO's panel spells "Rader". The sensor type and the claim are BRINBO's.

## Not getting stuck

Adjustable bars on the underside are set to avoid getting stuck on a pool step or ledge. A bar you can set is a sensible answer to the main hazard for a skimmer in a pool with a shallow shelf.

## Two speeds

Two cleaning speeds are set from one button, one press for slow and two for fast, or from the app. A skimmer with two speeds lets you trade coverage against battery, though BRINBO gives no figures for either.

## What we cannot tell you

- **Runtime,** basket volume, mesh size, warranty, weight and the largest pool it is rated for. None appear in what we read.
- **How it holds up.** 28 ratings is too few to tell a good machine from a lucky batch, and the product is marked as new for 2026.
- **The brand.** We found no track record for BRINBO in pool robots, so support and parts are unknown.
- **The colour.** Ours is the Black Blue listing.

## The verdict

The SK01 is worth a look if the app and the published charge time appeal, and the price is right. It is the one of the four we would wait on for more owner reports, because the numbers that matter most, runtime and basket size, are missing and the rating rests on a small sample. A rating this good from so few buyers is encouraging and proves nothing yet.
