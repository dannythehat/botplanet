## Who this is for

Someone finding out whether they want one of these at all.

That is a real buyer and nobody writes for them. A window robot is an odd
purchase: you cannot judge from a specification whether it will change your
Saturday or sit in a cupboard. The W1 PRO is the cheapest way to answer that
question from a manufacturer with a proper service operation behind it, rather
than from whatever is cheapest on Amazon this week.

It is also the right machine for ordinary framed windows on the ground and first
floor, cleaned every few weeks rather than constantly.

## Who should look elsewhere

Large panes. 2,800 Pa is the lowest maximum suction of any WINBOT and a big
sheet of glass is where that shows — the machine has further to travel and more
weight of water on board while it does it.

Anyone who wants scheduling, mapping or a serious app experience. Three modes is
three modes. WIN-SLAM 3.0 is two generations behind the WINBOT W3 Omni.

Anyone above the first floor who wants the longest safety margin available.
ECOVACS states eight-tier protection here against ten on the WINBOT W2 PRO and
twelve on the flagships, and — this is the part that matters — it states a
power-off hold without giving a figure for it. Every other machine in the range
gets a published 30 minutes. This one gets the claim and no number.

## Half the suction, and what that actually costs you

2,800 Pa maximum. ECOVACS does not publish a separate moving figure for the
W1 PRO, which the W2 PRO, the W2 PRO Omni, the W3 Omni and the Mini all get.

The moving number is the one that decides whether a robot holds glass while it
travels, so its absence here is more than a formatting gap. What we can say is
that the maximum is roughly half the W2 PRO's and well under the Mini's 7,500,
and that ECOVACS still rates the machine for framed and unframed glass.

The dual cross nozzle against six spray nozzles is the other real difference.
Fewer nozzles means the water lands in a narrower pattern, and spread is most of
what separates a clean pane from a streaked one.

## Streaking

The complaint people actually have about this category is not that the robot
fell off. It is that the window looks worse in low sun than it did before.

Spray pattern, cloth condition and how much water the machine puts down all feed
into that, and the W1 PRO is the least equipped machine here on the first of
those three. It is not a reason to avoid it on a normal window. It is a reason
not to expect flagship results from an entry machine, and to keep the cloths
clean, which matters more than any specification on this page.

## The verdict

Buy it to find out whether you want one, and buy it for ordinary framed windows
you can reach.

Do not buy it as a cheaper flagship, because it is not one — half the suction,
three modes, and a safety figure the maker declines to print. If the machine
earns its place in your routine, the WINBOT W2 PRO is what you replace it with,
and you will have spent less finding that out than most people spend guessing.
