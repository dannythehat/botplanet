Casa Leo makes one thing and this is it. Leo's Loo Too is $599, sits between
the $509.99 PETKIT and the $699 Litter-Robot, and does something neither of
them does: it works with a cat as light as one pound.

That single figure is the reason this page exists. Every other automatic litter
box in our catalogue detects its occupant by weight and gives up somewhere
around three pounds, which means a kitten simply does not register.

## Who should not buy this

Anyone with a large cat, and we have corrected our own record on this.

Casa Leo's own words are "spacious drum accommodates cats up to 20 lbs (9 kg)".
A male Maine Coon routinely passes twenty pounds. Until 10 August 2026 our
catalogue had this box marked as suitable for large cats, and because it was
tied on every other recorded attribute with the Litter-Robot and the PETKIT, it
was the machine our matcher named for large-cat queries — on an alphabetical
tie-break, against the maker's own stated ceiling. That was wrong, it is
corrected, and if you were about to buy on the strength of that
recommendation, the Litter-Robot 4 at 3 to 25 lb is where you should be
looking.

Anyone who will not use clay. Casa Leo requires 100% clay-clumping litter and
states that other litters void both the warranty and the 90-day trial. Any
brand is fine — Dr. Elsey's Ultra is their own recommendation — but the type is
not negotiable, and voiding a trial by using walnut litter is an expensive way
to find that out.

## The kitten case, which is the whole argument

"Our system works with cats as light as 1 lb."

Whisker's minimum is 3 lb. PETKIT's is 3.3. Below those thresholds the machine
does not know a cat is inside, and a cleaning cycle starting while a kitten is
in the drum is the hazard every weight sensor in this category exists to
prevent.

So if you have a kitten now, there are two honest answers: buy this one, or use
an ordinary tray until the animal is over three pounds and then buy whichever
box suits the adult cat. The second is cheaper and there is no shame in it. The
first is right if you want the box in place from the start and you are not
buying for a cat that will grow past twenty pounds.

## What $599 buys besides the sensor

A 9.5-litre waste drawer, which is the largest of the four boxes here — bigger
than the PETKIT's 8 litres, and a real figure rather than the Litter-Robot's
"as low as once every 8 days", which depends on cats.

Wi-Fi with app and voice control, weight tracking per visit, UV odor control,
safety sensors, and a running noise Casa Leo puts at about 30 dB. A one-year
warranty with a three-year extension sold separately, which is the same
arrangement Whisker offers and the same thing it tells you.

Four hundred and eleven Amazon ratings at 4.0, read 10 August 2026 — the
largest review pool of the four, which for a single-product brand is worth
something.

## What we could not find out

Casa Leo publishes a weight range, a drawer volume, a noise figure, a litter
requirement and a warranty. It does not publish an entry diameter, a drum
interior diameter or overall dimensions anywhere we could read on 10 August
2026, and its main domain timed out entirely — everything here comes from
casaleopet.com.

The entry size is the gap that matters. It is the specification that decides
whether a cat walks in or refuses, the Litter-Robot and the PETKIT both publish
theirs, and Casa Leo does not. For a 20 lb cat approaching the stated ceiling
that absence is not a detail, and it is another reason this is not the box for
a large animal.

We also could not establish whether any app feature sits behind a subscription.
Nothing on the page mentions one, which is not the same as a statement that
there is none.

## Who should buy one

Somebody with a kitten, or a small adult cat, who wants the box in place now
and will use clay litter. It is the only machine in the category that can
detect the animal, and the drawer is the biggest here.

Anybody with a cat heading past twenty pounds should buy the Litter-Robot 4.
Anybody with two or more similar-sized cats who wants to know which is which
should buy the PETKIT. And anybody who wants this box specifically should know
that the one figure deciding whether their cat uses it is the one figure Casa
Leo does not print.
