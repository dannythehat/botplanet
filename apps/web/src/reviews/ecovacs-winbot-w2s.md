The W2S is the W2 with different edge hardware and one fewer thing to explain.
ECOVACS gives it TruEdge scrubbers, WIN-SLAM 4.0 navigation and ten protection
stages — the same navigation and the same stage count as the WINBOT W2 PRO.

## Who this is for

Anyone whose complaint about window cleaning is the edges.

The middle of a pane is the easy part. What people notice, and what makes a
window look uncleaned from across the room, is the border where the glass meets
the frame — the strip a cloth reaches last and a robot's circular path reaches
worst. TruEdge is ECOVACS's answer to that, and it is what separates this
machine from the W2 PRO rather than any difference in grip.

Three spray nozzles against the W2 PRO's six, so the water goes down in a
narrower pattern and the scrubbers do more of the work.

## Who should look elsewhere

Anyone who wants the strongest specification in the W2 family. The plain W2 PRO
publishes 5,300 Pa ±500 maximum and 2,800 Pa moving; for the W2S we have no
published suction figure at all, which is not the same as it being weaker but
is not nothing either.

Anyone with sockets nowhere near their windows — that is the WINBOT W2 PRO Omni
and only the Omni.

Anyone with small panes, where the WINBOT Mini both fits better and, on
ECOVACS's own numbers, grips harder.

## Three names, one letter apart

ECOVACS sells the W2 PRO, the W2 PRO Omni and the W2S, and the difference
between them on a listing title is a word.

- **W2 PRO** — mains, six nozzles, seven modes, 5,300 Pa ±500.
- **W2 PRO Omni** — the same robot plus a 5.5 kg battery station.
- **W2S** — TruEdge scrubbers, three nozzles, WIN-SLAM 4.0, ten stages.

Check the details table on whatever you buy names the exact machine. This is
the single easiest mistake to make in the ECOVACS range and it is not a cheap
one.

## Frameless glass

Rated for it, like every WINBOT. ECOVACS supports frameless glass across the
whole range, from the Mini upwards, which means the category's supposed hard
exclusion is not an exclusion here at all.

If your glass is unframed, that removes the constraint rather than deciding the
purchase. Choose on edges, panes and price instead.

## What we cannot tell you

**Suction, either figure.** ECOVACS publishes maximum and moving suction for the
W3 Omni, the W2 PRO Omni, the W2 PRO, the W1 PRO and the Mini. For the W2S we
found neither. On a machine whose siblings are all documented, that gap is
conspicuous, and we would rather print it than infer a number from the W2 PRO
because the names look similar.

**Tank size, weight and dimensions.** Same situation.

**Whether the listing is this machine.** ASIN captured from an Amazon search on
5 August 2026, not machine-read for Brand and Model Number.

**How well TruEdge actually works.** It is the reason to buy this over the W2
PRO and we have not put one on a window. Nobody independent appears to have
measured edge coverage in this category at all.

## The verdict

Buy it if the edges are what bother you and you are willing to take ECOVACS's
word on TruEdge, because there is no independent evidence either way.

Buy the WINBOT W2 PRO if you want the better-documented machine. It publishes
its suction, its tank, its weight and its dimensions, and on a purchase where
the numbers are most of what you can judge, a maker that prints them is worth
something on its own.
