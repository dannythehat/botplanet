The EBO SE is the cheapest driving pet camera Enabot sells, at $119, and the
only one small enough to matter.

3.8 inches square and 3.5 tall. It goes under a sofa, which is where cats are.

## What you give up against the Air 2

**1080p instead of 2K**, and no emoticon face. That is the list.

Everything else survives the $30 saving: it drives from the app, talks both
ways, sees in the dark, has a 360-degree field of view and returns to its dock
when the battery is low.

Whether 1080p matters depends entirely on what you watch it on. On a phone,
rarely. On a laptop, sometimes. If you plan to squint at a corner of a dark
room to work out what the dog is chewing, buy the 2K.

## The size is the actual argument

Enabot's own copy calls it "compact design, no hidden spots", and that is not
marketing for once — it is 3.5 inches tall.

The places a pet goes when you are out are under the bed, behind the sofa and
into the gap beside the washing machine. A camera that cannot follow them there
is a camera pointed at an empty room. This is the model in the range that
follows.

## No subscription, and it is worth repeating

None. Charge it, install the app, scan a QR code, done.

Furbo and Petcube both sell monthly plans for features this gives away. In a
category where the recurring fee is the business model, Enabot not having one
is the reason to look at the brand at all.

## Who this is for

Somebody finding out whether a driving camera is useful. At $119 the answer is
cheap, and if it turns out you want 2K or a treat dispenser you have not spent
much learning that.

Also anybody with a cat. Cats go under things, and this is the model that goes
under things.

## Who should look elsewhere

**Anyone with stairs between them and the animal.** It has wheels. Nothing in
this category climbs.

**Anyone who wants to reward the dog.** That is the ROLA PetPal at $179.

**Anyone watching on a big screen.** 1080p driven around a dim room is not a
lot of pixels. The Air 2 is $30 away.

## Where this sits in the range

The SE is the cheapest and smallest of seven machines Enabot sells. The Enabot
range page puts all of them in order with what each one adds, which is the
faster way to check you are not buying the wrong end of the line.

## What we cannot tell you

**Battery runtime.** 2,500 mAh published, no duration anywhere we could read.

**Whether the 1080p bothers people in practice.** We have not lived with one,
and every review that has is comparing it against a phone screen rather than
against the Air 2.
