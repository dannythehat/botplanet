Four C batteries, no app, no account, no charging dock. The Joy For All
Companion Pet Cat is the least technological thing on this site and the one
most likely to be used every day by the person it was bought for.

It costs $159 and it has 12,307 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5, which is more
real feedback than every other robot pet in this catalogue put together.

## Who this is for

An older person, and specifically one living with memory loss.

Ageless Innovation has sold these into care settings for years and Amazon's own
age field on the listing reads "Seniors" — a rare case of a manufacturer naming
its buyer instead of widening the net. The design follows from that. There is
nothing to charge, nothing to pair, nothing to update, and nothing that stops
working when a company changes its mind.

Also the family member buying from a distance who will not be there to fix it.
That is the real test this product passes and most of the category fails.

## What it actually does

Sensors respond to touch and movement. It turns its head, moves a paw, meows,
and purrs in a way you feel through the fur rather than hear.

That is the whole behaviour list. It does not learn, it does not recognise
faces, it does not have moods that develop over weeks, and it will not know
your name. What it does instead is respond every single time, the same way,
which for a person with dementia is the point rather than a limitation.

The purr is the part people describe as the reason it works. It is a physical
sensation in a lap, and it does not require anybody to understand what the
thing is.

## The batteries are a feature, not a compromise

4 × 1.5V C alkaline, included.

A rechargeable robot pet needs somebody to remember the dock. In a house where
the whole reason for the purchase is that remembering is hard, a charging
routine is a failure mode with a battery indicator on it. C cells last months
and a relative can change them on a visit.

Nothing else in this category has understood that as clearly.

## Who should look elsewhere

**Anyone who would find a pretend cat patronising.** This is a real reaction,
it is more common than the marketing admits, and it is worth asking before you
spend $159. Ask the person, not their family.

**Anyone expecting conversation.** There is no voice, no assistant, no
questions answered. For a talking desktop companion robot the Eilik and the
Vector 2.0 are the machines, and neither suits this reader.

**Anyone who wants it to develop.** The Moflin changes over weeks and this does
not. That is a genuine trade and which side of it you want depends entirely on
who is holding it.

**A child.** It is built for an adult lap at 1 kg and just over 15 inches, and
a child will be bored inside a week.

## The thing nobody tells you about the fur

Synthetic fur over plastic, and Amazon's own materials row says so.

It looks convincing in photographs and it feels like a soft toy in the hand,
because that is what it is. Anybody expecting the weight and warmth of an
animal will notice immediately. Anybody who wanted something to stroke will
not care.

There is no subscription, no companion app and no account, so there is nothing
here that can be withdrawn later.

## What we cannot tell you

**Which colourway you receive.** Ageless Innovation sells the cat in several
and a dog as well. Our buy button points at B017JQQ00Q, the Silver with White
Mitts, identified by part number B7594. Other colours are separate listings at
prices we have not read.

**How long it lasts.** Twelve thousand ratings tell you people like it. They do
not tell you how many are still working after three years, and the
manufacturer publishes no figure.

**Whether it helps.** Care settings use these and the reported responses are
positive, but we have run no trial and we are not going to pretend a
comparison site can answer a clinical question.

## The verdict

Buy it for an older relative, especially one living with memory loss, and buy
it knowing exactly what it is: a soft toy with sensors, made well, sold
honestly, by a company that has been doing this longer than anybody else here.

The reason it is the pick on our robotic pets for elderly guide is not that it
is the most capable robot pet. It is that it is the only one whose design
decisions all point at the same person, and that person is the one you are
buying for.
