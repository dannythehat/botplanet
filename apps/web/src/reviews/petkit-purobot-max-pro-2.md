The Purobot Max Pro 2 is the only litter box in our catalogue that knows which
cat just used it. A 210-degree camera and facial recognition give it individual
profiles for up to fifteen animals, and it analyses the clump for urine pH
rather than only weighing what went in.

It is $509.99, which is $190 less than the Litter-Robot 4. It carries a two-year
warranty against Whisker's one. And it has an entrance five inches narrower
than the box it undercuts, which is the catch.

## Who should not buy this

Anyone with a big cat. PETKIT states 3.3 to 22 lb, which sounds close to the
Litter-Robot's 3 to 25 — but the entrance is 10.51 inches wide and 10.03 inches
high, against 15.75 inches square. A 22 lb cat that fits the weight sensor may
still decide that hole is not for it, and a litter box a cat refuses is worse
than no litter box at all.

The word "Max" in the name refers to the cylinder, which is 76 litres. It does
not refer to the door.

Anyone with a kitten under 3.3 lb, for the same reason as the Whisker: below
the sensor threshold the machine cannot know the cat is inside. Casa Leo's box
works from 1 lb and is the answer there.

## What multi-cat recognition actually buys

Fifteen cats, by face and by weight, each with its own health profile. In a
two-cat house the ordinary weight-only box has a real problem: two cats within
a pound of each other are indistinguishable, so the visit log is a single
smeared line and the early warning it promises never arrives.

Face recognition fixes that, and it is the thing worth paying for here. PETKIT
also records soft-stool instances and detects yowling — both of which are the
sort of feature that sounds like a gimmick until a vet asks you when it
started.

What to do with that: if you have one cat, this capability is worth nothing to
you and the Litter-Robot's larger door is the better $190. If you have two or
more of similar size, this is the only box in the category that can tell you
which one is off.

## The subscription, stated plainly

Live monitoring works with no subscription. Thirty days of video playback come
free. Extended recording needs PETKIT Care+ at a monthly fee.

That is a better free tier than Whisker's, which gives seven days of history
and gates two years behind Whisker+. Both companies are selling the same thing
in the end — the long view of your cat's habits is the paid product — and
PETKIT is at least clear about which part it is.

## Litter, safety and the things PETKIT publishes that others do not

It takes 99% of clumping litters — clay, tofu or mixed — provided particles
stay under 12 mm long and 3 mm across, and it ships with two sifters so you can
switch between tofu and bentonite. That is the widest litter compatibility of
the four boxes here, and the opposite end of the scale from the PetSafe, which
works only with PetSafe's own crystal trays.

Twelve safety sensors, a patented anti-pinch design that keeps the entrance
open at all times, weight sensors that stop the cycle when a cat is inside, and
proximity detection. Whether twelve sensors is more meaningful than four is not
something we can test from here, but PETKIT is the only maker of the four that
publishes a count at all.

## What we could not find out

PETKIT publishes more than the rest of this category — weight range, entrance
dimensions, cylinder volume, waste bin volume, litter particle limits, unit
weight, model number, warranty. It does not publish a decibel figure or a cycle
time, and those are recorded as not disclosed.

We also cannot tell you how a 22 lb cat gets on with a 10-inch door, because
nobody publishes that and it is exactly the sort of thing a specification sheet
cannot answer. The figures are here so you can hold them against your own
animal.

The rating is the thinnest of the four: 4.0 stars from 45 ratings, read on 10
August 2026, against 411 for the Casa Leo and 156 for the Litter-Robot. It is a
newer machine and forty-five people is not much to go on.

## Who should buy one

A multi-cat household with average-sized cats who wants to know which animal is
which. Nothing else in the category does that, the two-year warranty is the
longest here, and it is $190 cheaper than the box it is competing with.

One cat, or a big cat, and the Litter-Robot 4's door is worth the extra money.
A kitten and it is the Casa Leo. A tight budget and no interest in any of this
and it is the PetSafe, with its own caveats.
