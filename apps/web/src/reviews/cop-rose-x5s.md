The cheapest machine in this catalogue, and the only one with no app at all. It

It is also the only window robot we hold that is rated for framed glass only.

## Who this is for

Someone who does not want another app on their phone.

That is a real and slightly under-served position. A window robot is a machine
you use perhaps monthly, and pairing it, updating it and finding the right
screen every time is friction that a physical remote does not have. Hand a
remote to somebody who does not want a smart home and they can use it
immediately.

It is also the obvious first purchase for anyone who suspects they will not
actually use one of these often enough to justify spending more.

## Who should look elsewhere

**Anyone with frameless glass.** This is the hard one. Every ECOVACS in this
catalogue — from the WINBOT Mini upwards — is rated for framed and unframed
panes. The Cop Rose is rated for framed glass only, and that is not a
specification to argue with. A robot working an unframed pane has to detect an
edge it cannot feel, and this machine does not claim to.

If your windows have no frames, the shortlist does not include this product at
any price.

Anyone who wants scheduling, mapping or a cleaning history. There is no app to
put them in.

Anyone with sloped glass, which is the HUTT S55 Pro alone.

## No app is a genuine trade, both ways

What you lose: scheduling, remote start, cleaning modes chosen from a phone,
firmware updates, and any record of what the machine did.

What you gain: nothing to pair, nothing to update, nothing to stop working when
the manufacturer retires an app. That last one is not hypothetical in cheap
consumer robotics — an app-dependent machine from a company that leaves the
market becomes considerably less useful, and a remote-controlled one does not
change at all.

For a machine at this price, bought to clean a handful of framed windows every
few weeks, the second column is a reasonable thing to prefer.

## The verdict

Buy it for framed windows, on a budget, if you actively do not want an app.
Those three conditions together describe a real buyer and this is a sensible
machine for them.

Do not buy it for frameless glass — it does not claim it, and that is the one
line in this review that is not a matter of judgement. If your glass is
unframed and your budget is tight, the WINBOT Mini is the cheapest machine here
that is actually rated for the job, and on ECOVACS's own numbers it grips harder
than machines costing three times as much.
