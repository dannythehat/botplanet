The 298 is the cheaper HOBOT and the machine we know least about in this entire
catalogue. It uses the same ultrasonic spray as the HOBOT 2S and costs
meaningfully less.

We are going to be blunt about what that means: this review is shorter than the
others because there is less published to write about, and padding it would
misrepresent how much we actually know.

## Who this is for

Someone who wants ultrasonic spray at the lower end of the range, and who is
comfortable buying on a brand's reputation rather than on published numbers.

HOBOT has been in this category a long time and the 298 has been sold for
years, which is worth something — a machine that has been on the market that
long and is still listed has not been quietly withdrawn after a wave of
failures.

## Who should look elsewhere

Almost anyone buying their first window robot.

Not because we think the machine is bad. Because at a similar price the WINBOT
W1 PRO publishes its suction, its tank, its weight, its dimensions, its
navigation generation and its protection tier, and the 298 publishes
essentially none of that in a form we could verify. When two machines cost
about the same and one of them will tell you what it is, that is a real
difference.

Anyone with sloped glass wants the HUTT S55 Pro. Anyone with no socket near the
window wants the WINBOT W2 PRO Omni.

## Ultrasonic spray at the cheap end

The mechanism is the same idea as the 2S: water is vibrated into a fine mist
rather than pumped through nozzles, on the theory that an even film streaks
less than uneven droplets.

What the 298 does not appear to have is the 2S's pair of replaceable tanks,
which was that machine's clearest practical advantage. Take away the tanks and
the ultrasonic system on its own is an unmeasured claim rather than a feature
you can plan your afternoon around.

## What we cannot tell you

This is the longest section on the page and that is the honest shape of it.

**No suction figure.** Not maximum, not moving. Grip is the whole safety story
on a window robot.

**No power-off hold.** ECOVACS states 30 minutes on every model in its range,
including the cheapest. We have no figure for this machine.

**No tank volume, no weight, no dimensions, no navigation version, no warranty
term.**

**No manufacturer-verified specification of any kind.** Everything we hold is
retailer-stated. We read the ECOVACS numbers off ecovacs.com on 5 August 2026
and found no equivalent page for the 298.

**Identity not verified.** Owner-supplied ASIN from 5 August 2026, not
machine-read for Brand and Model Number.

## The verdict

It is in the catalogue because HOBOT should be represented and because a cheap
ultrasonic machine is a legitimate thing to want. It is not a machine we would
put in front of somebody who asked us what to buy.

If the ultrasonic spray is what draws you, the HOBOT 2S is the better version
of the same idea and its two tanks are a concrete advantage rather than a
claim. If the price is what draws you, the WINBOT W1 PRO costs about the same
and will at least tell you what it is.
