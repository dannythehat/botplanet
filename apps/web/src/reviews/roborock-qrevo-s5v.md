"Roborock Qrevo" is not a product. It is a family of at least four current
machines running from $499.98 to $879.99, and the difference between the
cheapest and the dearest is real.

This page is about the S5V, which is the one Amazon puts at the top of that
search and the one at the bottom of the price range. Twelve thousand Pascals,
10mm of mop lifting, a self-emptying dock and obstacle avoidance, for five
hundred dollars.

## What "Qrevo" covers, so you buy the right one

The S5V at $499.98. The Qrevo Series at $649.99. The Qrevo Edge 2 at $879.99,
which claims 25,000 Pa and a 3.14-inch body. The Qrevo 2 Pro at $799.99 with
20,000 Pa.

Four machines, one name in the search box, a three hundred and eighty dollar
spread. If you have been comparing "the Qrevo" against something else, you have
been comparing a range against a product, and the answer changes depending on
which end of the range you meant.

## The FlexiArm edge, and the ten millimetres

Roborock's FlexiArm swings the side brush and the mop out past the body so the
machine can reach a skirting board rather than leaving a stripe along it. That
is the S5V's headline feature and it is a genuine one — the strip along the
edge of the room is where a robot vacuum most obviously fails a visual
inspection.

Ten millimetres of mop lift is the other number to hold on to. It is two
millimetres less than the eufy X10 Pro Omni's twelve and half the S8 Max
Ultra's twenty, and it is enough for a fitted carpet and a thin rug. It is not
enough for anything thicker, and roborock makes no high-pile claim for this
machine.

## Two listings, one machine, and this one matters

B0DSP8J476 and B0FX4SZ4KB are the same Qrevo S5V at the same $499.98, sharing
one review pool of 1,625. A variation family again.

Here that is worth more care than usual, because the family this listing sits
in is a price range rather than a colour choice. A link that does not pin the
variant can move you along a shelf where the next machine along is three
hundred dollars more. Check the model string on the page you land on, not the
one you left.

## What we could not find out

The Amazon listing states suction, lift height, the dual anti-tangle system,
the mop washing and drying and the self-emptying. It does not state a runtime,
a bin capacity, a water tank volume, a noise figure or dimensions, and none of
those appear on any page we could read, so they are recorded as not disclosed.

We also cannot tell you how the S5V compares against its own siblings on
anything except price and the figures each listing chooses to print. Roborock
does not publish a comparison table across the Qrevo line, and building one out
of four differently-worded listings would be our guess dressed as their
specification.

## Who should buy one

Somebody who wants the roborock dock and edge-reaching behaviour at the
cheapest point roborock sells it, on hard floor with ordinary carpet. Five
hundred dollars buys the same four capabilities as the eufy X10 Pro Omni for
fifty more, with a better edge reach and a smaller review pool behind it.

Anyone who cares mainly about the review pool should take the eufy. Anyone
looking at the dearer Qrevos should be clear about which one they are reading
about, because the name on the box is the same on all four.
