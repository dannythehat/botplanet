### Why the separate best-of page is gone

The first draft of this list proposed a second page at
`/best-robots/window-cleaning-robots/` for `best window cleaning robot`. That
was wrong, and the evidence from our own run says so:

- **6 of the top 10 results are identical** between `window cleaning robot`
  and `best window cleaning robot`.
- **NYTimes ranks #1 for both** — with one article, not two.
- `best robot window cleaner` overlaps the best-of SERP **10/10**.
- `window cleaning robot comparison` overlaps it **8/10**.

Google is serving one result set. Two pages would compete for it, and the
weaker one would lose to our own stronger one.

This is the same test that refused `best budget`, `best cordless`, `best for
high rise` and `best for frameless glass` — applied one level higher, where it
should have been applied first.

**So the hub carries the "best" job itself**: a ranked pick list is a section
of the category page, not a separate URL. Combined, that single page targets
22,900 searches a month instead of splitting 12,100 and 1,300 across two.

**HOBOT S7 Pro was here until 2026-08-05.** It carried the highest Hobot
volume in the research (130/mo) and is not sold on Amazon US in any form —
neither is a plain S7. A review needs a working buy button, so it is dropped
and the WINBOT Mini takes the slot. HOBOT is represented by the 2S and 298.

**Why the last three matter more than their volume suggests.** BotMatch is a
rule-out engine: it needs a machine to offer when the reader's answer excludes
everything else. The Windowmate is the clearest case — it is a *different
class* (magnetic, both sides at once), the way the Betta SE Plus is a skimmer
rather than a pool cleaner. A reader with sealed double-glazing has no suction
robot that fits, and right now BotMatch would have nothing to say to them.
