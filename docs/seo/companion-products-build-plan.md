**Moflin is first despite not being the biggest**, because it is the only
product here that does not peak at Christmas. It runs 720 in July and **14,800
in September**; `casio moflin` peaks the same month at 4,400. That peak is
about four weeks out and it is the only deadline on this list that cannot be
moved.

**Everything else is a Christmas category.** `miko robot` runs 2,400 in June
and 40,500 in December — a seventeen-fold swing, matched by `miko mini` and
`miko 3` at 22,200 each, with `vector robot` at 22,200, `eilik robot` at 14,800
and `loona robot` at 9,900. A page published in November arrives at the peak
with no history. Live and indexed by mid-October is the whole game.

**Miko before Vector** even though Vector is 9,900 against Miko 3's 4,400:
KD 3 against KD 23, and Miko's range carries `miko robot` at 8,100/KD 10 and
`miko mini` at 5,400/KD 0 behind it. Easiest large thing first.

## How these have to be written

**Reddit is the competition.** `discussions_and_forums` appears on the review
SERP for Vector, Eilik, Loona and Moflin, and Reddit ranks third on every one:
"Thinking about getting a vector bot — Worth it?", "Is this worth buying?",
"A Dad's Review of Loona: Fun for a few hours!", "Is the Moflin worth it?".
Specifications do not beat a person saying whether they regret it. Every one of
these pages needs a verdict with a reason and an honest section on who should
not buy.

**keyirobot.com is the site to beat.** It appears in nine of the twenty-two
SERPs measured, including for `vector robot`, `joy for all companion pet`,
`robotic pet for elderly` and `best robot pet for adults` — products it does
not make. It is KEYi's content marketing, KEYi make Loona, and it currently
out-ranks us across this whole category.

**Miko Mini — not buyable, and it is the expensive answer.** 5,400/mo at KD 0
and the only thing Amazon US stocks is `B0FNW5YFYQ`, "LTGEM Case Compatible
with Miko Mini AI Robot for Kids (Case Only)". A case for a product is not the
product — the same pattern as Cozmo's $19 battery. **No Miko Mini page.** The
Miko 3 review names the Mini and links nowhere, because there is nowhere to
link.

**Miko 4 — nothing on Amazon and probably nothing anywhere.** 260/mo at KD 0
is what anticipatory searching looks like. Not planned.

**Ropet — buyable, confirmed, and it is a seventh product.** `B0GTPZ4N4M`,
"ropet KAMOMO Companion Interactive Robot Pet, Emotional Support for Kids and
Adults", $299, in stock. Searchers named it themselves in `moflin vs ropet`.
**No measured volume yet** — it was not in the seed inventory because nothing
knew it existed. It goes into the next paid run before it gets a page.

**Eilik DQ — buyable, confirmed, and it changes page 4.** `B0DBVM5BCY`,
"ENERGIZE LAB Eilik DQ — Touch Interactive Toys, Cute Robot Pets with Abundant
Emotions", $199.98. The earlier discovery pass reported it unmatched because
the listing title failed to parse, not because it was absent.

Two more Energize Lab products surfaced alongside it: **Panxer** (`B0DL6B8Q4D`,
"Eilik's Adventure Vehicle") and the **Eilik AI Station** (`B0FL2D6W4R`), plus
**Eiliko**, which ranks organically on `eilik price` and has its own Amazon
mystery-box listing. `eilik ai station` measures 260/mo at KD 0.

**This makes Eilik a range, not a product**, exactly as Miko is. Page 4 gets a
variants-and-accessories section covering DQ, AI Station, Panxer and Eiliko
with the DQ carrying its own offer, rather than a single-product review that
pretends the other four do not exist.

**Also found: a second Miko 3 listing**, `B0GV2L2PDL`, alongside the
`B0GV37M678` already recorded. Two ASINs for the same machine. The offer
register takes one and only one; which is the live buy box needs checking
before the page publishes.
