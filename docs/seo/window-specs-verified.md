### Findings worth a section in the reviews

**The cheapest machine grips hardest.** The WINBOT Mini claims **7,500 Pa max**
against the $499 W2 PRO Omni's 5,500 and the $380 W2 PRO's 5,300. Only the
$550 W3 Omni beats it, at 10,000. Suction is not what the money buys in this
range — nozzles, modes, navigation and the station are.

**Every ECOVACS in the lineup supports frameless glass.** The category's
supposed hard exclusion is not an ECOVACS exclusion at all. That materially
changes the frameless guide: the question is not *whether* but *how well*, and
the W1 PRO's answer — a coupler sensor reacting in 0.02 s — is the specific
claim to test.

**Power-off hold is 30 minutes across the range**, including the Mini. That is
the honest answer to "what happens in a power cut", and it is the same answer
at $150 as at $550.

**W2 PRO is heavier than W2 PRO Omni** (1.8 kg vs 1.6 kg) despite being the
cheaper, station-less model. Recorded because it is counterintuitive and a
reader lifting one to a first-floor window will care.
