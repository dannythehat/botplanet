## Froplay — refused, and the seasonality is the whole argument

`froplay dog reviews` came back from the first companion run at 2,900/mo and
KD 0, unidentified, and it looked like a free 2,900. It is not.

| Month | Jul | Aug | Sep | Oct | **Nov** | Dec | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | May | Jun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| froplay | 10 | **0** | 20 | 10 | **27,100** | 14,800 | 2,400 | 880 | 390 | 320 | 90 | 90 |
| froplay dog | 0 | **0** | 0 | 0 | **27,100** | 14,800 | 4,400 | 1,300 | 720 | 590 | 110 | 40 |
| froplay dog reviews | 0 | **0** | 0 | 0 | 6,600 | **22,200** | 3,600 | 1,300 | 390 | 140 | 50 | 50 |

Zero for four months, 27,100 in November, gone by June. That is an advertising
campaign, not a product with demand. The "2,900" is an annual average of a
spike that has already collapsed, and **today the term is at zero**.

The SERP says what the traffic is doing:

- Trustpilot — customer service reviews of froplay.com
- r/ScamsEtc — "Most Realistic Dog Toy Scam"
- YouTube — "Don't Buy AI Puppies"
- Facebook — "Is the robot dog a scam?"
- Amazon — "songbeat Froplay Robot Dog", a third-party seller name

`froplay scam` measures 70/mo and `are froplay dogs legit` 10. People are not
shopping; they are checking whether they have been had.

**BotPlanet does not build this page.** We would be ranking for scam-check
traffic on a product we cannot verify, do not sell and would not recommend.
The volume is real for six weeks a year and it is the wrong volume.

Recorded as a refusal rather than a gap so nobody re-opens it next November
when the number spikes again.
