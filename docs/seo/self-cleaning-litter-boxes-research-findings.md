## Two things worth knowing

**The brand is bigger than the category.** `litter robot` is **165,000/mo** — 50%
above the category term — and Whisker holds positions one and two with its own two
domains. It shares 6 domains with the head term, so it is the same family. It is not
a term we take off its owner. Secondary mention and a review page; never the H1.

**Safety is the story, not a caveat.** *"Do vets recommend self-cleaning litter
boxes?"* appeared in Google's People Also Ask box on **six of the twenty-three**
SERPs bought — the strongest repeated signal in any BotPlanet run so far. PETA ranks
page one for `are self cleaning litter boxes safe` with "3 Reasons Not to Buy a
Self-Cleaning Litter Box". The safety section is the most prominent thing on the page
and the hero's second CTA points straight at it.
