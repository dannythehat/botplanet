## The answer to the question that was asked

> **Are `pet camera robot` and `companion robot` one query family or two?**

**Two. Two URLs.** The evidence is not close.

| Pair | Shared top-10 domains | Which ones |
|---|---|---|
| `companion robot` vs `pet camera robot` | **2** | amazon.com, reddit.com |
| `companion robot` vs `robot pet camera` | **3** | amazon.com, reddit.com, youtube.com |
| `companion robot` vs `best pet camera robot` | **3** | reddit.com, amazon.com, youtube.com |

Every shared domain is Amazon, Reddit or YouTube — the three domains that
appear on almost every query in this category. **Discount those and the overlap
is zero.** Not one publisher, not one manufacturer, not one retailer in common.

The contrast is what makes it conclusive. Each family is internally cohesive on
domains that are *specific* to it:

| Pair | Shared | Distinctive domains |
|---|---|---|
| `pet camera robot` vs `robot pet camera` | **6** | store.enabot.com, wired.com, ebay.com, walmart.com |
| `pet camera robot` vs `best pet camera robot` | **5** | store.enabot.com, wired.com, gearbrain.com |
| `companion robot` vs `ai companion robot` | **6** | newatlas.com, en.wikipedia.org, globaltimes.cn, sixthtone.com |

Same shelf to us. Different SERPs to Google, and it is not marginal.

**The seasonality says the same thing independently.** Everything else in this
category peaks in November and December. `pet camera robot` peaks in **July**
(1,000) and troughs in **April** (140). People buy a companion robot as a
Christmas present and a pet camera robot before a summer holiday. Two products,
two occasions, two pages.

### Two warnings about these numbers

**KD is unreliable here and I am not going to pretend otherwise.** The bulk
difficulty call returned **7 for `robot dog` and 56 for `robotic dog`** — two
terms Google Ads clusters at the identical 90,500 volume, meaning it is serving
substantially the same SERP. It returned **6 for `companion robot` and 47 for
`companion robots`**. Those pairs cannot both be right. Treat KD in this
category as a rough band, not a figure, and let the SERP composition decide
difficulty instead. Where a ruling below depends on difficulty, it is argued
from who actually ranks, not from KD.

**61 of the 146 seeds returned no volume at all** — not zero, *no data*, meaning
they sit below Google Ads' reporting floor. That includes every consideration
question I seeded (`do companion robots need wifi`, `companion robot privacy`,
`companion robot subscription fee`, `what happens when a companion robot company
shuts down`) and every buying-guide phrasing (`how to choose a companion robot`,
`which companion robot should i buy`, `best companion robots 2026`). This is a
category whose long tail does not exist as search demand. It is a head-term and
brand-name category. That shapes the page list below more than any single
volume figure.

## 2. Seasonality — this is a Christmas category, and one page isn't

The curve matters more here than in any category BotPlanet has built. Pool
peaked 9× between June and December; several terms here beat that.

| Term | Peak | Trough | Multiple |
|---|---|---|---|
| tombot jennie | Jan 9,900 | Jul 320 | **31×** |
| robotic puppy | Nov 49,500 | Jun 1,900 | **26×** |
| miko mini robot | Dec 5,400 | May 260 | **21×** |
| miko 3 | Dec 22,200 | Jun 1,300 | **17×** |
| emo robot | Dec 40,500 | Jul 8,100 | 5× |
| robot dog | Nov 165,000 | Jun 40,500 | 4× |
| companion robot | Nov 6,600 | Feb 2,900 | 2.3× |
| **pet camera robot** | **Jul 1,000** | **Apr 140** | **7×, inverted** |

**Three consequences.**

1. **The build deadline is real.** Gift-intent traffic starts ramping in
   October and peaks in the last week of November. A page published in December
   has missed the year. Today is 6 August. Everything in the companion and
   product list below needs to be live and indexed by **1 October 2026**.
2. **`pet camera robot` runs on the opposite calendar** and is already past its
   2026 peak. There is no deadline pressure on that page, which is convenient,
   because it is also the page with the least traffic to win.
3. **The averages lie in both directions.** `companion robot` reads as 4,400 but
   is 2,900 for five months of the year. `tombot jennie` reads as 2,400 and is
   320 in July. Do not plan capacity or forecast revenue off the annual figure.

## 3. Rulings — one per candidate page

### ✅ CREATE — `/robots/companion-robots/` (the hub)

**Primary intent: `robot pet` / `robotic pet` — 8,100, CPC $0.70, KD 0.**
**Not `companion robot`.** That is a deliberate reversal of the obvious choice
and the SERPs are the reason.

`companion robot` (4,400) returns:

> en.wikipedia.org · **globaltimes.cn** · reddit *(Ubtech humanoid launch)* ·
> ortomi.com · amazon.com · **newatlas.com** *("World's 1st mass-produced
> humanoid robot")* · reddit *(same Ubtech thread)* · **sixthtone.com**
> *("China's Lifelike Emotional Companion Bots Go on Sale")* · youtube
> *(UBTECH U1)* · housebots.com

Three Chinese tech-news outlets and two humanoid-robot launch stories. That is a
**news SERP about humanoid robots**, not a consumer commerce SERP. Two of the
ten results are anything we could compete with.

It is also **intent-contaminated**. The related-keyword pull off `companion
robot` returned `companion robot woman` (390) and `ai companion robot for
adults` (320); the `ai companion robot` SERP carries a Facebook result reading
*"It's an endless love. It will never betray you."*, and its People Also Ask
includes *"What is the AI robot companion for adults?"*. A meaningful slice of
this head term is AI-girlfriend intent. We do not serve it and should not build
an H1 that courts it.

`robot pet` (8,100, KD 0) returns the opposite — a clean product SERP:

> **store.enabot.com** *(ROLA PetPal)* · **living.ai** *(EMO)* · reddit ·
> youtube · **amazon.com** *(Loona)* · keyirobot.com · shop.elephantrobotics.com
> · en.wikipedia.org · **us.aibo.com**

Manufacturers and retailers, twice the volume, zero difficulty, and every
product on it is one we would stock.

**Keep the URL `/robots/companion-robots/`** — the slug keys nav, routes, the D1
category row and BotMatch, and renaming it is a change to live registries for no
SEO gain. But the **H1, title and primary register entry lead on robot pets**,
with `companion robot` served as a secondary term in the body.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | `robot pet` (8,100) |
| **Secondary** | `robotic pet` (8,100), `companion robot` (4,400), `ai companion robot` (1,900), `robot friend` (1,000), `robot pets for adults` (480), `desktop companion robot` (480), `interactive robot companion` (260) |
| **Cedes** | `pet camera robot` and its family → `/robots/pet-camera-robots/`; `robotic pet for elderly` and its family → the seniors guide; `robot dog` → nothing, refused outright |
| **Evidence** | `robot pet` SERP is manufacturers + retailers; `companion robot` SERP is 3 news outlets + 2 humanoid launches. Head terms share 4 domains, all universal (reddit, youtube, amazon, wikipedia). |

**Why one hub and not two, given `companion robot` and `robot pet` only share 4
domains.** The head terms diverge because `companion robot` is polluted by
humanoid news and adult-companion intent — not because buyers want different
things. The *commercial* variants converge: `best companion robot` vs `best
robot pet` share **5** (reddit, keyirobot.com, amazon, youtube, robotshop.com),
which meets the one-URL threshold. Buyers land in the same place; only the
news-driven head term wanders off. One hub, built on the term that converts.

### ✅ CREATE — `/robots/pet-camera-robots/`

**Primary intent: `pet camera robot` — 480, CPC $1.24, KD 9.**

Split from the hub on the evidence at the top of this document: zero distinctive

> **gearbrain.com** *(EBO Air review)* · videostrong.com · **ebay.com**

One manufacturer holds three of ten positions. There is exactly one major
publisher (Wired) and one niche one (GearBrain). That is a page we can win.

buy a different SERP — it buys Wirecutter and Furbo as competitors for ten
searches a month. The unmodified `pet camera robot` is the whole opportunity.

### ⛔ REFUSE — pages for the consideration questions

Every question I seeded came back with **no volume data**: `do companion robots
need wifi`, `companion robot privacy`, `companion robot subscription fee`, `how
long do companion robot batteries last`, `can a robot pet climb stairs`, `do
companion robots record you`. `do robot pets help dementia` returned an **empty
SERP** — no organic results and no features at all, which is not evidence either

subscription?"*. Google is telling us what buyers are afraid of, three products
running.

## 6. Two process problems this run exposed

**The research was bought twice today.** Run `31080749246` completed at 07:29
on `research/companion-robots` and uploaded results; run `31081889310` repeated
the work at 07:46. Combined spend is roughly $0.45 against a $2.00 cap. The
cause was that the artifact from the first run could not be read — this
environment has no artifact-download tool and the GitHub REST API is blocked —
so the numbers existed and were unreachable. **Fixed:** the workflow now runs
`scripts/seo-research-digest.mjs`, which prints volume, CPC, KD, the twelve-month
curve, related terms, all ten organic domains per SERP and the pairwise
domain-overlap table into the job log. It spends nothing and is
`continue-on-error`, so it can never fail a paid run. Every finding in this
document was read out of that log.

**Two seed inventories exist for one category**, on two branches, and the paid
runs used different ones. That breaks the audit trail the process depends on —
"if a term is not in the file, it was not considered" only works when there is
one file. **These must be merged into a single inventory before any further
spend on this category.**
