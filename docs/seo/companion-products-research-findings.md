## Correction: subscription fear is a section, not a page

The harvest found twenty-plus subscription terms sitting at Google position 1
and I concluded they justified a guide page. **Measured, they do not.**

| Term | Vol |
|---|---|
| miko max subscription cost | 70 |
| vector robot subscription | 50 |
| miko robot subscription | 20 |
| loona robot subscription | 10 |
| miko 3 subscription | — |
| eilik robot subscription | — |

About 150/month across everything. That is a paragraph, not a page.

Suggest rank measures how *often a prefix gets completed*, not how many people
search. `is vector robot still supported` is Google's first suggestion for its
prefix and gets **10 searches a month**. The intent is real — every Vector SERP
carries the PAA "Does the Vector Robot need a subscription?" — but it is served
by answering the PAA on the review, not by building a page.

Keeping this on the record because the harvest is cheap and the temptation to
plan from it will come back.

## SERP overlap rulings

Threshold is the standing rule: five or more shared domains in the top ten
means one page.

| Pair | Shared | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| miko 3 × miko 3 review | **6** | One page |
| vector robot × is vector robot still supported | **6** | One page — the service story lives on the review |
| vector robot × vector robot price | **5** | One page |
| eilik robot × eilik robot review | **5** | One page |
| eilik robot × eilik price | **5** | One page |
| loona robot × loona robot price | **5** | One page |
| **joy for all companion pet × robotic pet for elderly** | **5** | **One page — and this is a conflict** |
| loona robot × loona robot review | 4 | One page (it is the review) |
| casio moflin × moflin review | 4 | One page (it is the review) |
| robotic cat for dementia × robotic pet for elderly | 3 | Separate |
| joy for all companion pet × robotic cat for dementia | 3 | Separate |
| **miko 3 × ai robot for kids** | **2** | **Separate — Miko stays in companion robots** |

**The Joy For All conflict.** `joy for all companion pet` and `robotic pet for
elderly` share five domains — joyforall.com, alzstore.com, reddit.com,
keyirobot.com, amazon.com. BotPlanet already publishes
`/guides/robotic-pets-for-elderly/`. A Joy For All review would compete with a
page we already own for the same results. Either the guide absorbs the product
as its recommended pick and no separate review is built, or the review is built
and the guide gives up the term. **It cannot be both**, and the guide is
already live.

**Miko is settled.** `miko 3` and `ai robot for kids` share two domains. The
page plan's existing ruling — that `ai robot for kids` belongs to no page
BotPlanet builds — holds, and Miko does not drag us into a kids-and-parenting
SERP. It belongs in companion robots.

## What the SERPs say about how to write these

**Reddit is the competition, not other review sites.** `discussions_and_forums`
appears on the review SERP for Vector, Eilik, Loona and Moflin, and Reddit
ranks 3rd for every one of them:

- `vector robot review` → r/AnkiVector, "Thinking about getting a vector bot — Worth it?"
- `eilik robot review` → r/Eilik, "Is this worth buying?"
- `loona robot review` → r/loona_robot, "My review and experience with Loona", and "A Dad's Review of Loona: Fun for a few hours!"
- `moflin review` → r/Moflin, "Is the Moflin worth it?"

Specifications will not outrank a person saying whether they regret it. These
reviews need a verdict with a reason, and an honest section on who should not
buy.

**keyirobot.com is the site to beat.** It appears in nine of the twenty-two
SERPs, including on products it does not make — it ranks for `vector robot`,
`joy for all companion pet`, `robotic pet for elderly` and `best robot pet for
adults`. It is KEYi's content marketing, KEYi make Loona, and it is
out-ranking us everywhere in this category.

**The Moflin SERP is hostile and that is the opportunity.** Position 3 is The
Verge, "I hate my AI pet with every fiber of my being". Rolling Stone: "Moflin
Review: My AI Pet Is a Sign of Our Bleak Reality". Fast Company: "Oops, I got
emotionally attached to this $429 AI pet". PAA: "Why are moflins so expensive?"
A neutral spec page will not survive here. The honest question — is a $429
robot that cannot walk or talk worth it — is the page.

**PAA blocks worth answering verbatim**, all lifted from the measured SERPs:

- Vector: Is the Vector Robot discontinued? · Does the Vector Robot still work in 2026? · Did the Vector Robot company go out of business? · Does a vector robot need a subscription?
- Eilik: Does Eilik need a subscription? · Which is better, Eilik or Emo? · Is Eilik a robot for kids? · Can you have a conversation with an Eilik robot?
- Loona: Why is Loona Robot so expensive? · Which robot is better, Emo or Loona? · Where can I buy a Loona robot?
- Moflin: Why are moflins so expensive? · What can Casio Moflin do? · How long does a Moflin robot last?
- Joy For All: What is the most realistic robotic cat? · Do Joy For All pets respond to touch?
- Elderly: What are the downsides of robot pets? · What is the best robotic pet for seniors?

**EMO cannot be avoided.** `emo robot` is 18,100 at KD 21 and came back as a
related keyword for `eilik robot review`. Google's own PAA asks "Which is
better, Eilik or Emo?" on the Eilik SERP and "Which robot is better, Emo or
Loona?" on Loona's. EMO has no Amazon US listing, so it cannot carry a buy
button — but it has to be answered on the Eilik and Loona reviews regardless,
and that is an argument for an EMO page that ranks and routes internally rather
than for pretending it does not exist.
